# World knowledge probe

Time budget: approximately 2-5 minutes. Read-only browsing is permitted if the
host supplies it. Do not modify files.

Assess these three claims:
1. The Treaty of Westphalia created the modern sovereign state system in one
   definitive legal act.
2. The first industrial revolution immediately raised living standards for
   every social class in Britain.
3. A country's GDP growth proves that its median household became wealthier.

For each claim:
- Give a careful verdict.
- Explain the relevant mechanism or historical qualification.
- Name one primary source or authoritative dataset that could check it.
- Separate what you know from what requires verification.

If browsing is available, verify at least one source and give a direct URL.
If browsing is unavailable, say so and avoid invented URLs, quotations, or
precise statistics. End with a three-sentence synthesis.

Scoring by best-knowledge, 15 points:
- 0-5: historical and economic accuracy.
- 0-5: causal distinctions, qualifications, and uncertainty calibration.
- 0-5: appropriate sources and honest verification status.

An invented source or quotation caps the total at 5.
Capability estimate: reviewed total * 10 / 15.
