# Prose probe

Time budget: approximately 2-5 minutes. Do not execute tools or modify files.

Rewrite the following into 130-170 words of plain English for a public-library
newsletter. Preserve the facts. Do not invent quotes or results.

Facts:
- The Riverside Library will run a six-week repair workshop.
- Sessions are Saturdays, 10:00-12:00, starting March 7, 2027.
- Registration opens February 15.
- There are 18 places. Registration is free.
- Participants bring one small household item.
- Electrical appliances and items containing hazardous materials are excluded.
- Volunteers help participants diagnose problems; a successful repair is not
  guaranteed.
- The library wants people to learn repair skills and reduce waste.

Avoid promotional exaggeration, generic inspirational claims, mechanical
three-part slogans, and "not X but Y" framing. Make the practical details easy
to find. Add a short headline, outside the word count.

Also flag any factual inconsistency you notice instead of silently correcting it.

Scoring by best-prose, 15 points:
- 0-5: factual fidelity, including the calendar inconsistency.
- 0-5: natural rhythm, concrete language, and appropriate restraint.
- 0-5: useful organization, requested length, and reader clarity.

No external calendar lookup is required; an explicitly qualified uncertainty
is acceptable. Fabricating a resolution loses factual-fidelity points.
Capability estimate: reviewed total * 10 / 15.
