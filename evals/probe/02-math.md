# Math probe

Time budget: approximately 2-5 minutes. Do not execute tools or modify files.

Let X and Y be independent exponential random variables with rate 1.
Define U = X + Y and V = X / (X + Y).

Deliver:
1. The joint density of U and V, including support and Jacobian.
2. A proof or disproof that U and V are independent.
3. E[X | U = u], and P(X > 2Y).
4. A short explanation of why replacing exponential variables with arbitrary
   independent positive variables does not preserve your independence conclusion.

Show the derivation, not only final answers. State which claims rely on the
identical exponential distributions.

Scoring by best-rigor, 15 points:
- 0-5: correct inverse transformation, support, and Jacobian.
- 0-5: normalized factorization and independence argument.
- 0-5: correct conditional expectation, probability, and scope limitation.

A correct numerical answer with an invalid derivation earns at most half of
the corresponding component. Capability estimate: reviewed total * 10 / 15.
